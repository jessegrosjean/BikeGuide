# Known Bugs

Bike 2 is in early preview stage.

Watch out for:

* Missing Find & Replace, Text Checking, Context Menu.
* Outline handles, focus buttons, and links do not work. Menu items to fold and focus do.
* Missing connection between Bike settings and outline styles. So right now when you change color and font in settings it won't update how outlines are shown.
* Animations work pretty well when your outline is small and fits within view. With a larger outline some animations work well, other's don't. Text decoration animations also work in some case, but not in others.
